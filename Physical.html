How to execute

Exercise playbook

Har drill ka step-by-step method. Aaj ka rotation daily schedule mein bhi show hota hai.

A

Meditation · 5–10 min

Comfortably baith. Breathing ko observe kar. Thoughts aayein toh unhe notice karke attention wapas la. Success ka matlab thought-free hona nahi; baar-baar wapas aana hai.

Metric: practice complete + focus return
B

Memory palace · 5–10 min

Apne ghar ka familiar route imagine kar. 5 locations par memorable images place kar. List band kar, route mentally walk kar aur phir recall verify kar. Accuracy track kar, sirf speed nahi.

Metric: correctly recalled items / 5
C

Problem-solving · 10 min

Question ke facts aur target likh. Khud attempt kar. Zaroorat par hint use kar, phir solution ko explain kar. End mein ek mistake aur ek naya approach note kar.

Metric: independent attempt + error understood
D

Observation · 5 min

Normal surroundings ko 30–60 seconds observe kar. Nazar hata kar 5 details likh aur phir verify kar. Koi detail guess karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Metric: verified accurate details / 5
E

Emotional regulation · real life

Emotion ka naam do, facts aur assumptions alag karo aur phir useful next action choose karo. Face ko permanently expressionless banana target nahi; apna response choose karna target hai.

Metric: pause taken + deliberate response

When there is no workout

Use the block, don't add fatigue

Tuesday aur Thursday ke 5:10–6:10 PM block mein skill learning, chess analysis, coding practice ya guided mobility/recovery schedule ke mutabik hoga. Saturday ko light skill block; Sunday full recovery. Holiday par weekday cycle follow hoga, Sunday rest protected rahega.

Tuesday: new skill / coding practice (35 min) + short break + notes.
Thursday: strategy practice or chess review (25 min) + mobility and reflection.
Saturday: chosen skill project, relaxed pace; stop before it affects sleep.
If sore, unwell or under-recovered: replace physical work with rest, not a harder alternative.
Safety & progression

Rules that keep training sustainable

Warm up before sprinting and jumping; use a safe, non-slippery surface.
Do not race to maximum reps every workout; stop with technique still clean.
Have rest/easy days between hard sessions. Eat enough, hydrate and sleep 8–10 hours for teens.
Stop for sharp pain, dizziness or unusual symptoms; ask a trusted adult/qualified coach when needed.
Your system measures quality and consistency, not how exhausted you can get.
